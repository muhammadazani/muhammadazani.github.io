Curriculum Vitae

Muhammad Azani Hasibuan, M.T.I., PMP · Lecturer in Information Systems · PhD Candidate in Computer Science

Lecturer in Information Systems at Telkom University and PhD candidate at the University of Western Australia. I combine more than fifteen years of experience in university teaching, learning technology leadership and IT consulting. My research applies learning analytics, data mining and process mining to understand and support students’ self-regulated learning in online and collaborative environments.
